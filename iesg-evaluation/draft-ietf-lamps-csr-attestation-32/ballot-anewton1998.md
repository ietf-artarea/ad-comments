## Comments

### Working Groups Don't Live Forever

Supposedly, the RATS working group won't be around forever, or could be superseded
by MORERATS or RATSWITHLASERS or whatever, and the importance of the relationship
is to the group's output, not the group itself.

148	2.  Relationship to the IETF RATS Working Group

150	   As noted, attestation-related technologies have existed for many
151	   years, albeit with no standard format and no standard means of
152	   conveying attestation statements to a CA.  This draft addresses the
153	   latter, and is equally applicable to standard and proprietary
154	   attestation formats.  The IETF Remote Attestation Procedures (RATS)
155	   working group is addressing the former.  In [RFC9334], RATS defined
156	   vocabulary, architecture, and usage patterns related to the practice
157	   of generating and verifying attestations.


The following is offered as a suggestion:

     2.  Relationship to RFC9334

     In [RFC9334], RATS defined
     vocabulary, architecture, and usage patterns related to the practice
     of generating and verifying attestations.
